# Coldbrew app surface

Mode: Operate. Daily workbench: Dashboard, Campaigns, Prospects, Outbox, Settings. Brand token: Coldbrew.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A slow-brewed workbench: calm, steady, nothing urgent unless it needs your approval; refuses the loud sales-dashboard (neon accents, red badges, confetti numbers).
OWN-WORLD: Espresso dark theme (#1B1410 ground, #261C16 panels, ink #F1E7DA) and a crema light theme (#F5EEE4 ground, #FFFBF5 panels, ink #2B1D14), same hues in both. Caramel (#D49A5A / #A86A2E) is the one action colour, sage for replies and good news, steam teal for information, brick only for do-not-contact and errors. Schibsted Grotesk throughout, tabular numerals in every table and chart. Left rail navigation. Charts are flat, ink-and-coffee ramps with direct labels, no gradients.
STORY: A person opens it with a coffee, sees in one glance what needs approval and what came back, then works the queue without hurry.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail (wordmark, five destinations, campaign picker at the foot). Main: a quiet header line with today's date and one sentence of state ("3 drafts wait for your approval, 2 follow-ups are due"), a row of six figures (prospects, in outbox, sent, replied, meetings, follow-ups), then a two-column grid of charts: activity over time (stacked bars) and the funnel, with reply rate by step and angle below.
FORM: Calm workbench, pinned by the owner (calm and steady, coffee colours); the direction roll was not used because the brief pins the aesthetic.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
